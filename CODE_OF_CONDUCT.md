# Code of conduct

Be respectful and keep discussions focused on the work. Contributors and maintainers must not harass, threaten, insult, discriminate against, or expose private information about other people.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Explain the evidence behind your position, assume good faith, and give people room to correct mistakes. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, reject contributions, or block participation when behavior makes the project unsafe or unproductive.

For a private conduct report, use the repository's [Security Advisory form](https://github.com/hshokrig/opencode-bedrock/security/advisories/new) and state that the report concerns community conduct. Include links or screenshots needed to review the incident. Do not use a public issue when the report contains personal or sensitive information.
